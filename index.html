HOW TO PLAY

SCRABBLE SINGLE is a daily seeded solo word challenge. Every player receives the exact same sequence of 35 tiles today. Build the highest-scoring board before time or tiles run out!

Core Rules
  • First Turn: Must cover the center star ★ (counts as a 2W Double Word score).
  • 7-Tile Rack: After each valid word, replacement tiles are drawn strictly in order from today's hidden 35-tile queue.
  • 7-Tile Bingo: Playing all 7 rack tiles in a single turn awards a +35 point bonus!
  • Clear the Rack Bonus: Using every tile in the queue and emptying your rack awards +25 points!
Swapping, Discarding & Penalties
  • Swap (⇄): Costs -3 pts per tile. Sends selected tiles to the very back of the hidden queue in order and draws replacements.
  • Discard / Burn (🗑): Costs minus the tile's face value (Blank = -2 pts). Permanently trashes selected tiles and draws replacements.
  • Endgame Rack Penalty: Any unplayed tiles left on your rack when the game ends deduct their face value.
Snappy Controls

• Drag & Drop tiles, or Tap a Tile then tap a board square.
• Keyboard Mode: Tap any board square to focus it (tap again to toggle → or ↓) and type letters directly from your rack!
